# Code of conduct

VisionOps contributors are expected to keep project spaces respectful,
constructive, and welcoming.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and
deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome;
focus criticism on ideas and observable behavior, assume good faith, and make
room for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when this policy is violated. Report conduct concerns
privately to the maintainers through the repository's contact or security
reporting channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
